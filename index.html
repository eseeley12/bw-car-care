The Studio

Luxury finishes, a professional high-end approach.

BW Car Care & Detailing was founded on a simple idea — every vehicle deserves the same care its owner would give it. Our new Ballina studio was built for one thing: high-end work. Full colour-change wraps, PPF, ceramic coatings and paint correction that hold up under close inspection.

Mobile to your door for the day-to-day, or booked into the studio for the big jobs — every panel gets the same meticulous, unhurried attention.

What We Do

Detailing & protection, done properly.

A complete range of high-end protection and presentation services — from a full interior refresh to a full colour-change wrap.

01

Ceramic Coating
